    How automated assistance works

    Evaluation drafts and initial feedback are generated by a Gemini AI model using instructor-verified selected diffs, documented responsibilities, and matching source at the pinned checkpoint. Relevant source is sent to your configured provider only after confirmation. The model cannot prove authorship, push time, compilation, runtime behavior, or visual quality. You are responsible for reviewing and editing the final feedback and score.

    Approve checkpoint evidence to enable suggestions.

    Checker setup and source-sharing details

    An instructor-managed Gemini provider key is required. It stays in this browser, not in student records. Only evaluate after approving source sharing; never put a key in feedback. Automated generation assists your review and may be inaccurate.
