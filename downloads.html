Launch Adminerva ↗
Browser installation options

Installation controls vary by browser and device. Check the steps below.

Windows / macOS browsers

  1. Open Adminerva in Chrome or Edge.
  2. If your browser offers Install app in the address bar or menu, select it.
  3. Otherwise, bookmark or pin the website for quick access.

Android browsers

  1. Open Adminerva in Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu and look for Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Follow the prompts if your browser offers installation.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Adminerva in Safari.
  2. Choose Share, then Add to Home Screen (sometimes under More).
  3. Confirm the shortcut or web-app option offered by your device.

Important: Installing the web experience does not install the future Adminerva Companion. Browser shortcuts do not gain permission to inspect other applications or silently capture screens.

Good to know

Do I need an account to visit this page?

No. This page is public. School records, assessments and staff tools still require the appropriate verified Adminerva account.

Can I download a Windows, Mac, Android or iOS app today?

Not yet. Official native builds and store listings will be linked here only after they are published and verified. The web version remains available now.

Will installing the website enable examination monitoring?

No. Web installation alone does not provide desktop application inspection. Any future exam-monitoring feature requires a separately enabled school policy, the relevant permissions and a visible notice.

Will the native app update itself?

Not yet. The unsigned developer previews can check the official version listing but cannot install updates automatically. Windows and macOS production apps will use verified, signed in-app updates; Android and iPhone/iPad releases will update through their approved stores. No update may interrupt an active examination or unconfirmed Studio cloud save. Do not uninstall Android previews simply to switch builds, because signing keys can differ and local data could be lost.

Will my saved work move between devices?

Use your verified school account and Adminerva's supported cloud-save workflows. Device-only drafts or unsynchronized work do not automatically appear on other devices.
