How practice and XP work

1. Choose an answer and explain your prediction. 2. Run the example in Code Playground. 3. Return here, describe the result, and save completion.

You need a verified student account linked by your school to an active student record and section. If your class is missing, ask your teacher to check your email and section in User Directory. Teachers can view class practice from Practice Analytics.

Each mission is learning practice, not a graded exam. Completed missions save automatically and earn 10 XP once per mission. Replays do not add XP. Practice XP is not a school grade or proof of who answered. Your class uses generated aliases on the leaderboard.

Draft reflections stay in this browser tab until submitted. Shared computers are supported: use “Continue practice on this device” if another practice session is active. Earlier self-recorded progress does not become verified XP.
