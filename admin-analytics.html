Retention and cleanup

Events and optional report trails carry a 30-day expiry. Expiry timestamps alone do not delete data. Automatic deletion needs separately approved Firestore TTL configuration. Until enabled, run cleanup regularly and repeat when batch limits are reached; arrange account-deletion requests with the administrator. Preferences and latest teacher summaries persist separately.
