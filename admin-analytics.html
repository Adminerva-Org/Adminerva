Automatic maintenance policy

Both options are off by default. Saving these settings does not deploy a worker. An administrator must separately approve the backend, school privacy policy and operating costs. The hourly worker only handles monitoring records and non-grading teacher summaries; it never changes or publishes grades.

When the consent handler is deployed, opt-outs remove the student's rows from the latest teacher snapshot independently of the hourly policy. Processing may be delayed; stopping sharing is immediate, but previously sent events still follow retention. Changes already in progress may finish.

Retention and cleanup

Events and optional report trails carry a 30-day expiry. Expiry timestamps alone do not delete data. The separately approved hourly worker removes at most 600 expired records per collection per run. A recorded cleanup cap means backlog may remain; use manual cleanup or separately approved Firestore TTL for larger volumes. Arrange account-deletion requests with the administrator. Preferences and latest teacher summaries persist separately.
