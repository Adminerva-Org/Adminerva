Coverage, retention & privacy

This log is for authorized system debugging, not student misconduct findings. A backend callable returning without an exception does not independently prove that a requested school record was committed. A normal click means it was observed, not that a save or submission succeeded. The Live console is a three-second polling view, not a direct socket or a guarantee of every interaction. Severity and module filters are applied server-side in the Global Event Stream; text search filters the currently loaded rows only. Events are recorded at server receipt time and may arrive late or be missing. No password, student source code, private notification body, full URL, raw stack trace, grade, screenshot or arbitrary typed input is collected. Previously recorded per-user events remain accessible under the original account timeline; the new global index starts with newly recorded events. Diagnostic records expire after 60 days. Global error-pattern counts are a bounded sample, not an exact platform-wide census.
